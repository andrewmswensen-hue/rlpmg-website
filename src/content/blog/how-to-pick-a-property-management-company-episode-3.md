In the previous RL Property Management episode #2 ([https://rlpmg.com/blog/how-to-pick-a-property-management-company/](/blog/how-to-pick-a-property-management-company/)) we covered how to evaluate the local property management company marketplace.

In the video below, Peter with RL Property Management covers step 3 of 5 that you can take to select a property manager for your property.

**Step 1: Determine your needs. (Video #1)**

**Step 2: Evaluate the marketplace. (Video #2)**

**Step 3: Research the top 10 and narrow down. (Video #3)**

**Step 4: Call 5 companies. (Future Video)**

**Step 5: Meet with 1 or 2. (Future Video)**

Video highlights:

1. Using your notes and findings from step #2, create a list of the 10 top property management companies in your area.

2. Complete a more thorough “due diligence” process for each company. You should find out as much as possible without engaging a salesperson.

- See if you can find any materials they have put out: blogs, videos, Instagram, tweets, e-books etc.
- Review their rental listings. Are they detailed? How are the photos?
- Consider driving by some properties they manage.
- Make sure you read reviews if you have not done so already. Keep in mind that angry customers will use reviews to vent.
- Search their name online. Check the biggerpockets.com real estate website to see if they are active.
- Try doing research on the names of the company owner / broker / principal.
- Are they licensed? Look them up on your State’s licensing website, if it exists.
- What is the companies primary focus?

3. Reminder, a PM company has the potential to affect the value of your asset. Would you buy a property without due diligence or an inspection?

4. Your goal here is to narrow down the list to top 3 to 5 (you will be calling the top companies in the next step).

5. With this information in hand, it should become clear that 3 to 5 companies are the clear leaders in the space. Jot down their numbers as you prepare for the next step “step #4 – phone calls”.
